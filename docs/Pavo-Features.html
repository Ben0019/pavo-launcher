15 · Quick controls

One swipe from the edge.
Everything, over the game.

A compact glass card that floats above the running app without pausing it. Brightness, volume, performance, fan and refresh rate sit in one reachable column, with capture actions along the bottom.

Panel

  • BRI / VOL — tall vertical sliders. Both write while you drag: no release step.
  • PERF — SMALL / MEDIUM / LARGE / STOCK with the tuner's own profile colours.
  • FAN — SMALL, MEDIUM, LARGE, HOLD, OFF, SMART, HIGH, CURVE in a 2×4 grid, coloured like Fan Control.
  • REF — Auto, 60 Hz and 120 Hz.
  • Chips wrap tightly, titles are centred, the card is sized to the two-column grid.

Actions

  • SHOT — full screenshot. Long press opens the region selector.
  • REC — whole-screen recording through the built-in recorder, started invisibly. Long press records a single app.
  • MAP — opens the in-game Touch Mapper overlay only, never the mapper app.
  • Mapping is bound to the app behind the panel: enabling or editing from here saves to that app's profile.
Region capture
  • Drag a rectangle over the frozen screen.
  • Move the box from inside, or drag any of the eight handles to resize.
  • Live size badge shows the exact pixel crop.
  • SAVE · DELETE · RESET · CANCEL bar, styled like the recorder HUD.
  • The bar dodges the selection, hopping to the other edge.
  • Saved to Pictures/Pavo Screenshots with a media scan and toast.

15 · Quick controls

Park the handle anywhere.

The grab handle is a small accessibility overlay that stays above other apps. It never appears in a screenshot or a recording, and it snaps back to its saved slot after a restart.

Handle

Right, left, top or bottom edge. Long-press, drag, release to snap.
Open

Size

10–100 %, live preview.

Show or hide it entirely from the same card.
Saved
Edge and slot are remembered; re-applied on every reboot.

Overlay & drawer card

The old overlay actions card is replaced by QUICK CONTROLS: a show/hide toggle and three sliders for the grab handle. The metrics card gives up room so the column still fits without clipping.

Gesture summary
  • Tap a control to act; long-press where a variant exists.
  • The panel dismisses itself before a capture so it never appears in the image.
  • Handle touches are excluded from the system back gesture.
Behaviour
  • The panel is a trusted accessibility overlay, so games that hide non-system overlays still show it.
  • Brightness and volume write through the privileged channel and the audio service; both are throttled to about 50 ms while dragging.
  • The mapper overlay opened from MAP keeps its own per-app layout — buttons, sticks, combos and zones are stored against the package.

11 · RGB lighting

Two rings. Two zones.
Full control.

The Odin 3 exposes one SN3112 LED driver per joystick. Each stick has a single live RGB zone, so the launcher paints two independent colours at once — one per hand — with every mode, colour and gesture available from the RGB app.

What the hardware can do

  • One addressable zone per stick; the second logical zone is not wired on the Odin 3.
  • Eight driver channels all address the same physical zone — no gradient around a single ring.
  • Two sticks, two colours simultaneously, each with its own mode.

Modes

  • STATIC fixed colour · BREATH speed-controlled pulse
  • RAINBOW hue rotation · STROBE on/off flashes
  • MULTI two hues, 120° apart · SCREEN follows the display
  • OFF disables that stick but keeps its colour

Per-stick editing

  • BOTH shares one colour and mode; switching adopts the selected stick's settings.
  • EACH gives each stick its own colour and mode; long-press toggles one stick off without forgetting its mode.
  • Editable hex field: #RRGGBB, RRGGBB and #RGB, with type, delete, copy, paste and cut. Invalid codes restore the last valid colour.
  • Controller: D-pad or left stick moves focus, A selects, hold A toggles the focused stick.

SCREEN mode

  • Left half of the display drives the left stick; right half the right stick.
  • 160 ms sampling with a 0.38 blend settles a scene change in roughly 0.25 s.
  • Dominant hue voting plus 22 % of the plain average keeps neutral areas grey and vivid accents vivid.
  • Pauses while the RGB app is open, so it never samples its own UI.
Mode behaviour
STATIC
Fixed colour; each stick keeps its own stored colour.
BREATH
Slow brightness pulse; speed controls the cycle, colour is preserved.
RAINBOW
Continuous hue rotation across the spectrum.
STROBE
On/off flashes at the speed setting.
MULTI
Two hues at once, one per stick, both cycling with a 120° offset.
SCREEN
Follows the display; each half drives its stick.
OFF
Disables that stick without forgetting its last colour or mode.

Accuracy choices

  • Dominant-hue voting ignores large neutral areas, so a vivid accent is not averaged into grey.
  • 22 % of the plain average is mixed back to keep the actual tint.
  • No palette cycling: the result is stable while the screen is stable.

What it cannot do

  • Show a spatial gradient around one ring — the ring is a single zone.
  • Show more than two simultaneous colours across both sticks.
  • Capture DRM-protected or secure surfaces.

The rest of Pavo

A complete handheld home

01Home screen

Paged icon grid, folders, resizable widgets, clock/weather/status header, transparent dock, layout backup and restore as a ZIP.

02App drawer & navigation

Full drawer, dock pop-up drawer, predictive back, install shortcuts, focus-first D-pad navigation.

03Appearance & wallpaper

Liquid-glass dock, drawers and sheets; wallpaper-derived accent, icon packs, per-surface transparency, OLED protection.

04Performance tuning

Per-cluster clocks, governors and GPU frequency table; presets and per-app profiles, background hold, thermal guard.

05Performance overlay & logs

CPU/GPU temperature, clock and load, RAM, FPS graph, per-core rows, battery temperature and power draw.

06Stress testing

CPU, GPU and combined runs with fixed clocks or a tuning profile; live graph, averages, maxima, throttle and hold figures.

07Fan control

Off, Low, Smart, High, Curve and Manual; 15-point curve, CPU/GPU/BOTH source, per-app profiles.

08Device health & charging

Battery model, health, cycles, resistance, USB details; 80 % limit with automatic charge separation.

09File manager

Dual panes for internal, SD and USB OTG; copy, cut, paste, rename, delete, ZIP, previews and text editing.

10FTP host & client

Share storage with read/write switches and QR login; mount a remote server as a pane with live previews.

11RGB lighting

Two independent zones, seven modes, per-stick editing, editable hex, gesture toggles, screen-follow colour.

12Controllers, overlays & shortcuts

System-wide remapping, per-app layouts, touch mapping, in-game overlay menu and physical shortcuts.

15Quick controls

Edge-handle panel with live brightness and volume, performance and fan presets, refresh rate, screenshots, region capture and recording.

14Languages, compatibility & limits

25 locales; Android 12+; 1920×1080 and 1280×960 with portrait fallback; clock and fan writes remain firmware requests.

Under the hood
  • Plain Java against the Android platform: no AndroidX, no Gradle runtime.
  • Built-in apps extend one glass activity and share the card material.
  • Per-app profiles for performance, fan and touch mappings.
  • PServer is the only privileged channel; nothing else asks for root.
  • 25 language catalogues are loaded at runtime, no resource rebuild.
  • Everything local — no accounts, no telemetry.

Controller & install

The ring never leaves you stranded

Home & drawers

  • A opens the focused app or activates the focused control.
  • Hold A opens the item menu: rename, icon, info, remove, folder.
  • B backs out of a menu, folder or drawer.
  • L1 / R1 previous and next page; also page edges in lists.
  • L2 dock pop-up drawer; R2 full drawer.

Quick controls

  • Swipe the edge handle inwards with a finger to open the panel.
  • Long-press the handle to drag it to another edge.
  • Long-press SHOT for a region, REC for single-app capture.

RGB & system shortcuts

  • A on a joystick selects it; Hold A toggles it off and on.
  • BTN_C floating side drawer; hold for its style menu.
  • BTN_Z performance overlay; hold for the overlay menu.
  • Perf Logs key — optional third shortcut.

Getting started

  • 01 Install Pavo.apk or run adb install -r Pavo.apk.
  • 02 Open Pavo once and grant or skip each permission in the setup sheet.
  • 03 Set Pavo as the home app, then press Home.

Compatibility & limits
  • Android 12+ (API 31), 25 languages, 1920×1080 and 1280×960 with portrait fallback.
  • Privileged writes use the vendor PServer channel; no su, Magisk or KernelSU dependency.
  • One RGB zone per stick: two simultaneous colours maximum; clock and fan writes can still be reduced by the vendor thermal manager.
Shortcuts at a glance
  • Edge handle — swipe inwards: panel. Long-press + drag: move it.
  • SHOT tap: screenshot. Long press: region.
  • REC tap: whole screen. Long press: single app.
  • MAP tap: in-game mapper overlay for the app behind the panel.
  • BTN_C drawer, hold for style. BTN_Z overlay, hold for its menu.
  • B closes the panel, the region selector and the mapper editor.
Where files go
  • Screenshots: Pictures/Pavo Screenshots
  • Region crops: …/Aurora_<time>_region.png
  • Recordings: Movies/Pavo Recordings
  • Single-app clips keep the app_ prefix.
  • All captures are media-scanned, so they appear in Gallery immediately.
  • Layout backups save as a setup ZIP.
